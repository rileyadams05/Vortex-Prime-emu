Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Information We Use

For Streamz Pro, Vortex Prime may store your internal account ID, Google stable subject identifier, Google email, Discord user ID used for verification, checkout contact email, full name, date of birth, Stripe PaymentIntent or customer identifiers, entitlement status, payment amount, currency, timestamps, and security or rate-limit records.

Payment Data

Stripe processes payment details. Vortex Prime does not receive or store raw card numbers, wallet credentials, CVC values, or bank details.

Discord Verification

Discord is used to claim a paid Streamz Pro verification session. The human-readable verification code and website token are temporary, single-use, and stored by Vortex Prime only as secure hashes.

Google Account Linking

The Streamz Pro licence is linked to Google's stable subject identifier after activation. Changing a visible email address does not move ownership of the licence.

Data Sharing

Vortex Prime uses service providers such as Stripe for payments, Discord for verification interactions, Google for sign-in, and Cloudflare or Google Drive-backed storage for backend operation. We do not sell Streamz Pro activation data.

Contact

If you need help with Streamz Pro account access or privacy questions, contact Vortex Prime support through the official website or Discord server.
