Why Stream with Vortex Prime?

Play your modded PC games on any console — no jailbreak, no homebrew, no fuss.

No Homebrew Required

Your PS5, Xbox, or Switch stays completely stock — no modifications, no warranty voiding, no bans. Just open the browser and connect.

Mods on Console

Console games can't run mods natively — but your PC can. Stream your modded PC games to your console and play with full mod support on the big screen.

Low Latency Local Streaming

Streams directly over your home network — no cloud, no relay, no added lag. Your PC and console talk directly for the fastest possible response time.

One-Time Setup

Install the app once and complete the Sunshine setup on your PC. Open the Streaming tab — your PC is detected automatically. If not, connect using your IPv4 address. No URLs. No complicated setup.

Performance Notice

Native PC games stream at up to 120 FPS. Emulated games are capped at 60 FPS for stability and device compatibility. 120 FPS requires a display and console that support 120 Hz output. The correct frame rate is applied automatically — no settings required.

Game Compatibility & Performance

The platform supports both native PC games and emulated console games within a single streaming interface. Performance is handled automatically based on the game type.

Native PC Games

Up to 120 FPS

Any standard Windows game (.exe) can be streamed directly. These games run through Sunshine and are not frame-rate limited by the system. Performance depends on your PC hardware and network conditions.

Emulator Games

Capped at 60 FPS

Emulated titles are launched from the same front-end and streamed through the same interface. They are capped at 60 FPS for stable performance and compatibility with console devices.

Automatic handling — the system detects the game type and applies the correct performance profile. No manual configuration is required.

If your console or display does not support 120 Hz, streaming will run at 60 FPS automatically regardless of game type.

PC Streaming Setup

To stream your PC through the website, Sunshine must be installed and running on your computer. Sunshine allows your PC to act as the streaming host so it can be detected by the Streaming tab.

1

Install the App

Download and install Vortex Prime on your PC. Sunshine is included and will be set up automatically as part of the installation.

2

Configure Sunshine

Launch Sunshine, complete the initial setup, and allow it through your firewall if prompted. Confirm it is running in the background.

3

Open the Streaming Tab

On your console or any device, open this website and go to the Streaming tab. Your PC will be detected automatically and shown as an available device.

4

Start Streaming

Select your detected PC and begin your streaming session directly from the browser.

If Your PC Is Not Detected

If automatic detection does not work, enter your PC's IPv4 address in the field above. Find your IP in the Vortex Prime app under the Streaming tab.

Important Notes

  • ✓ You do not need to paste or enter any URLs
