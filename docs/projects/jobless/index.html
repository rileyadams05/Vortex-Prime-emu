The project

A world built around choice, progression and consequence.

Jobless is an original Unreal Engine game being created by Vortex Prime. Its design combines an explorable open world with story-led missions, character progression and systems that let players approach objectives in different ways. Features, content and release timing may change during development.

01 / STORY

Story-driven play

Structured missions and player-led exploration designed to support a clear narrative journey.

02 / WORLD

Open exploration

An evolving environment with activities, progression systems and reasons to discover what is beyond the next block.

03 / PROGRESS

Long-term progression

Player growth and unlockable opportunities intended to make each session contribute to the wider experience.

Online features

Invite-based multiplayer, with Discord kept optional.

Planned multiplayer uses Epic Online Services for account sign-in, friends, invitations, lobbies and game sessions. Optional Discord connection may provide social presence and voice features. A Discord account is not required to play, and Discord availability does not control access to EOS multiplayer.

EOS

Game authority

Epic Online Services is planned to manage player identity, invitations, lobby membership and multiplayer sessions.

INVITES

Play with friends

Invite-based sessions are intended to make it straightforward to gather friends and enter the same game session.

DISCORD

Optional social layer

Players may choose to connect Discord for supported social or voice features without making it a multiplayer requirement.

Platform notice: PC (Windows) is the current development target. Additional platforms are being evaluated. Vortex Prime does not claim console certification, approval or a confirmed console release.
