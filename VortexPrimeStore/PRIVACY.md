# Privacy Policy

Vortex Prime Store is a website and creator portal for PlayStation homebrew, HEN/GoldHEN resources, store packages, tools, and mods.

## What We Collect

The public website can be browsed without signing in.

If you sign in with Google for creator/admin features, the site may use your Google profile information, such as:

- Display name
- Email address
- Profile image

This information is used to show your signed-in profile and support creator/admin workflows.

## Uploaded Store Data

Packages submitted through the creator portal may include:

- Package title
- Creator or author name
- Platform
- Tags
- Icon and preview images
- Package or archive files
- README or Markdown content
- YouTube links
- External source or download links

Only content intentionally submitted for publishing should be uploaded.

## Local Browser Storage

The website may use browser storage to remember sign-in profile details and creator/admin editing state. This helps keep the portal usable between page loads.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Public package metadata and uploaded content may be displayed on the Vortex Prime Store website once published.

Third-party services may be used for sign-in, hosting, or linked media. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Security

Do not upload private keys, passwords, personal documents, or sensitive files through the creator portal. Store submissions are intended to become public.
