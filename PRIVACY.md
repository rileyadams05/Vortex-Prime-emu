Overview
Vortex Prime ("the Application") is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Statement explains the personal data the Application processes, how it is processed, and for what purposes. This Application uses the Microsoft Identity Platform to provide a secure, authenticated experience for Xbox users.

 Data Collection and Usage
Vortex Prime interacts with your Microsoft Account to provide enhanced gaming features. We collect and use data for the following purposes:

Authentication & Profile: We use your Microsoft credentials solely for authentication through official Microsoft secure login pages. Vortex Prime never sees or stores your password.

Xbox Live Data: Upon your consent, we access your Gamertag, Gamerscore, and Achievements to display your gaming progress within the Vortex Prime dashboard.

Personalization: This data is used only to personalize your experience and provide real-time updates to your local emulator profile.

 Data Sharing and Third Parties
Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, license, or share your personal data with any third-party ad networks, data brokers, or monetization services.

Service Context: Data is used exclusively to deliver the "Vortex Prime" experience to you as the player.

 Storage and Security
Local Processing: Your Xbox profile data is processed locally on your device. We do not maintain external servers to store your private gaming history.

Encryption: All interactions with Microsoft services are conducted over industry-standard encrypted transport protocols (HTTPS/TLS).

 User Control and Deletion
Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw your consent and de-link your Xbox account at any time through your Microsoft Account security settings.

Data Deletion: If you remove the Application or de-link your account, all locally cached identifiers and profile data will be immediately deleted.
